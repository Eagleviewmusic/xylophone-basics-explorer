Putting a lesson in Google Sites

Press Copy link on a lesson, then in Google Sites: Insert → Embed → By URL, paste, and make the box wide (16:9 fits best). Add ?ex=3 to the link to open on exercise 3, or ?mallet=off to start with the student playing.
